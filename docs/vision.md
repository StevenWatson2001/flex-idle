# Vision

What Flex Idle should be when it's finished. Steven owns this file. Use it
to judge whether a feature moves the game toward this; the roadmap says
what comes next, this says where it ends up.

## The game in a paragraph
A lightweight fantasy incremental game in the style of Cookie Clicker and
Realm Grinder, built mainly as a test project for the learning platform.
You earn money, buy units and upgrades, and ascend to start again stronger.
The twist: each ascension, an AI creates a new unit or upgrade for you,
shaped by how you played, which you keep for every future run. The goal is
the usual incremental one: get the most money, collect every upgrade and
become absurdly powerful.

## What a player does
- Clicks for money; clicking stays a valid way to play, not just an early
  boost.
- Buys units for idle income and upgrades that multiply it. Choosing the
  right upgrades matters more than activity on screen.
- Ascends to reset a run in exchange for lasting progress, including a new
  AI-made unit or upgrade.
- The game is endless; milestones mark progress along the way.

## Where the AI comes in
- On each ascension, the AI generates one new unit or upgrade from that
  run: how it was played, how far it got, the rarity of recent upgrades,
  and a random modifier.
- Generated content belongs to the player who earned it and joins their
  pool alongside the hard-coded units and upgrades.
- Rarity matters, and rare results can be game-changing or even broken;
  that's part of the fun.
- Each generated unit and upgrade gets AI-made art in one simple,
  consistent art style.

## Look and feel
Clean panels, lists and big numbers, like Realm Grinder: little movement on
screen, with fantasy theming and the AI art carrying the character.
Desktop browser.

## What it is not
- Not multiplayer or social: it's a single-player game for Steven.
- Not busy or action-heavy.
- Not a balanced competitive game: broken rare content is allowed.
